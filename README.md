## About

I'm a front-end developer based in Iran. I wrote my first line of code, `print("Hello World")`, in 2019, during the early pandemic lockdowns, out of curiosity more than any real plan.

A year later, a group of friends showed me how to build websites with HTML and CSS. That became my focus, then my career.

My design approach blends modern technique, classic structure, and fluid motion. I build interfaces that hold up over time and still feel current years later.

I'm open to new projects and ideas worth building. Reach out if you have one.
